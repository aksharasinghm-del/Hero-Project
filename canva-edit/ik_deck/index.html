INSIGHT → IDEA
04
THE INSIGHT
"For now" lasts years. So renters live like guests in their own homes.
They postpone "real" furniture to a future that keeps moving. The result: IKEA's biggest growth audience buys the least.
THE IDEA
Home, for now.
Stop treating renting as waiting. IKEA becomes the brand that makes a temporary home feel permanent, and helps it move with you.
Democratic design, for renters
Circular by 2030
Life at home, as it really is
THREE PILLARS, THREE PRODUCTS
05
The campaign isn't an ad. It's a range, a kit and a service.
Deposit-Safe
Labelled range of freestanding, lean-on and plug-in pieces. In-store tag, online filter, landlord-proof guarantee.
Solves: the landlord
Night One
€199 kit (illustrative): bed, bedding, lamp, kitchen basics. Delivered the day you get the keys. Partner with letting agents.
Solves: the first week
Move-Out Day
Built on IKEA Buy Back & Resell: book a pickup, get store credit, your piece goes to the next renter.
Solves: the money
