03 · THE IDEA
04
The 100-Day Shift.
100 people who never sit down. 100 shifts in Allbirds. Every step counted. Their worn pairs become the campaign.
STEP 1 · RECRUIT
100 wearers
Nurses, cooks, baristas, teachers, dog walkers and new parents in NYC, Chicago and London.
STEP 2 · WEAR
100 shifts
Step counts and wear diaries, filmed by the wearers themselves. Real, not studio.
STEP 3 · REVEAL
The scuffs are the ad
Worn pairs photographed like museum pieces, then sent to ReRun for a second life.
